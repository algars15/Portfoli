About Me

Video Game Design and Development student specializing in 3D Art and Technical Level Design.

On the artistic side, my focus is on sculpting, high-to-low poly workflows, and PBR texturing to create cohesive, game-ready assets. On the engine side, I bridge art and gameplay by assembling levels, configuring shaders and materials, and implementing mechanics directly in Unity and Unreal Engine.

I possess extensive, demonstrable experience across Autodesk Maya, ZBrush, Substance 3D Painter, and Photoshop. I am seeking to apply my skills in a professional studio environment where I can contribute to ambitious productions and continue growing as a 3D artist.
